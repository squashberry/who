What WHO is checking

Useful context, not a verdict.

WHO is designed to surface whatever trustworthy caller context is available: possible identity, reputation signals, community reports and actions you can take.

SignalMeaning
IdentityPossible name or category associated with the number when available.
ReputationSpam, scam or community context when supported.
ConfidenceHow strongly the available signals support the result.
ActionAnswer, ignore, block or report based on what you see.
WHO app

The full result belongs on the phone.

The public website provides the search-first entry point. The live WHO caller-intelligence experience is currently centered in the Android app, where the incoming call, contact context and protection tools are available together.

Looking up a number here does not reveal a private person's identity by magic. Caller information can be incomplete, outdated or wrong.
