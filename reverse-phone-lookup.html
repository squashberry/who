Caller intelligence

Context inside the phone app.

WHO's caller intelligence is designed for incoming calls inside WHO. It is not a separate public reverse-phone-search service.

In the app

When a call arrives

WHO can look at the incoming number, your local contact information and available caller signals.

Privacy

No need to paste numbers into the site

The website is for product information and downloads. Caller intelligence belongs in the WHO phone experience, where the relevant permissions and controls are available.

Community

Signals can be reported

Users can report unwanted or suspicious calls. WHO's architecture is designed to aggregate signals rather than treat one report as unquestionable truth.

Beta

Expect the system to evolve

Caller matching and reputation features are actively developing during Beta 1.0. Results should be treated as useful context, not certainty.
