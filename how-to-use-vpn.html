How to use WHO VPN

WHO's network screen can establish a native Android VPN/tun2socks path for supported traffic and lets you inspect available connection information before you connect.

Step 1

Open WHO Network

Open WHO and go to the Network/VPN area. Review the current connection state before starting anything.

Step 2

Review the endpoint

When endpoint information is available, compare the country, protocol, latency and uptime shown by WHO. A lower latency is generally better for responsiveness, while uptime can help you avoid an unreliable route.

Step 3

Connect

Choose the endpoint or route you want and start the connection. Android may display a system VPN permission dialog the first time; approve it to allow WHO to create its VPN tunnel.

Step 4

Verify protected state

Return to the WHO Network screen and confirm the connection shows its active/protected state. If WHO reports disconnected, do not assume traffic is still using the tunnel.

Step 5

Use the phone normally

Once connected, use your apps as usual. WHO's native VPN path handles supported traffic through the selected route until you disconnect.

Step 6

Disconnect

Open WHO Network and stop the tunnel when you no longer need it. You can reconnect later and choose a different available route if one is provided.

What WHO VPN does — and does not do

Does

Creates an Android VPN tunnel for supported traffic and gives you connection state and endpoint information inside WHO.

Does not

A VPN does not make you anonymous, does not bypass every website's restrictions and does not make a suspicious website or download safe by itself.

VPN troubleshooting

Permission denied

Reopen WHO Network and accept Android's VPN permission prompt. Only one active VPN service may be allowed at a time, depending on your device.

Connection fails

Try another available endpoint, check your base internet connection and retry after stopping any other VPN app.

Slow connection

Check latency and choose a route with more responsive characteristics when alternatives are available.

Relay + VPN

When using WHO Relay, the receiving phone can use WHO's native VPN/tun2socks path for the relay session when that path is available.
