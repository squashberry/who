Caller ID

Know more before you answer.

WHO can combine the number arriving on your phone with local contacts and available caller-intelligence signals to give you useful context.

1 · Recognize

Start with your contacts

If the number belongs to someone saved on your phone, WHO can use that local identity as the most direct signal.

2 · Compare

Use available community signals

For unfamiliar numbers, WHO can compare the normalized number against anonymized caller observations and other available signals.

3 · Decide

Use the context, then choose

A caller match is context, not proof. Answer, ignore, block or report according to what you know about the call.

4 · Correct

Report bad information

When a caller identity or reputation signal is wrong, use WHO's reporting/correction path so future results can be improved.

Important

WHO is not a public website where you need to type a phone number just to learn about the app. Caller intelligence is designed as an in-app phone feature, with privacy controls around contributed signals.
