Caller ID

See useful context around an incoming number before you decide what to do.

Designed for quick decisions

WHO aims to show the most useful identity signal first, without burying a ringing caller under complicated screens.

Confidence matters

Where community or business signals are available, WHO should show confidence rather than imply certainty.

Your contacts come first

A caller can already be someone you saved. WHO should use the local contact context where appropriate before reaching for broader identity signals.
