Privacy Policy

How WHO plans to collect, use, protect and delete information while the product grows.

1. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Squashberry's WHO product may process information through the WHO mobile application, website and related services. WHO is currently a beta product, so some features described below are planned rather than active. When a new feature materially changes what information is processed, we intend to update this policy before enabling the feature.

2. Information you provide

  • Account and sign-in information such as your phone number, display name or authentication identifiers.
  • Feedback, support messages, reports and other information you choose to send to WHO.
  • Contact information you choose to manage or back up in the app.

3. Information created by using WHO

  • App version, operating-system version, device model and basic technical diagnostics.
  • Security and reliability events such as failed requests, crash information and abuse-prevention signals.
  • Caller/spam reports you intentionally submit.

4. Contacts and the future identity network

Your personal contacts are not automatically treated as public WHO records. The planned community identity feature may use user-authorized contact-derived observations to generate confidence-based candidate names. Before that feature is activated, WHO will provide a clearer product notice explaining what is processed, the purpose, the retention period and the controls available to users.

5. Google Drive backups

When contact backup is introduced, WHO will ask for Google authorization and use the permission needed for the backup. Your backup will be treated as your data, separate from the community identity dataset. You can revoke Google's authorization through your Google account.

6. How information is used

WHO may use information to provide features, identify spam, improve reliability, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, maintain security, enforce our terms and understand aggregated product usage.

7. Sharing

WHO is designed to minimize unnecessary sharing. Service providers may process information on WHO's behalf for hosting, authentication, infrastructure, analytics or support. We do not design the product around selling your private contact list.

8. Retention and deletion

We aim to keep information only for as long as needed for the stated purpose, legal requirements, security, dispute handling and legitimate operational needs. Specific retention periods may vary by data type and feature.

9. Your choices

You can request access, correction or deletion where applicable, withdraw optional permissions, and use the available identity/data controls described on the Your Data page.

Manage your data →

10. Children

WHO is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to lawfully use the relevant service in their jurisdiction.

Beta notice: this privacy policy is a product draft for WHO Beta 1.0 and should be reviewed and adapted for the final operating entity, jurisdictions and launched data flows before production release.
