How we use your information

A plain-language guide to the purposes behind WHO's data processing.

We use information for specific product purposes

PurposeExamples
Provide WHOAccount sign-in, caller features, contacts, SMS and settings.
Caller intelligenceCompare permitted identity signals, display candidate names and confidence.
Spam protectionProcess reports and reputation signals to help identify unwanted calls.
ReliabilityCrash diagnostics, performance events and service health.
SupportRespond to questions, feedback, correction and deletion requests.
SecurityDetect abuse, protect accounts and investigate suspicious activity.
Product improvementUse aggregated or appropriately minimized usage information to improve features.

Information we do not need to use

WHO should not need your password, one-time passcodes, bank PINs or unrelated sensitive documents to provide its core caller-ID experience. Do not send such material to support.

Your contacts

The product direction is to keep your personal contact list under your control. A future community matching feature may process limited contact-derived observations only after the relevant feature notice and permission flow are in place.

Number suggestions

A suggested caller name can be wrong. WHO is intended to represent identity as a confidence-based signal, not as a guaranteed legal identity record.

Backup

Google Drive backup is designed to be an explicit user action. The backup copy is separate from public-facing caller intelligence and should be encrypted before upload.
