WHO features

Calling, contacts, messages and caller intelligence in one consistent experience.

Caller ID

WHO is designed to show useful context around an incoming number when the service has an appropriate identity signal. Results should be presented with confidence and source context rather than treated as unquestionable facts.

Spam protection

Users can report unwanted numbers and, as the reputation system grows, WHO can surface warnings and blocking controls.

Calls & messaging

WHO brings dialer, recent calls, contacts and SMS into the same visual system.

Contacts

Manage your contacts locally, with future cloud backup and restore under your own authorization.

Planned

  • Community identity matching
  • Spam and scam reputation
  • Google Drive backup & restore
  • Expanded desktop support
  • More caller context and corrections

Platform availability

Android is the current development focus. iPhone, iPad, macOS and Windows are planned and will be announced when ready.
