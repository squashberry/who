Backups

WHO contact backup should be user-authorized and separate from community caller-intelligence observations. This area can later show backup/restore events without exposing the user's contact content to admins.

No backup service connected. Google Drive OAuth + per-user restore jobs will be implemented in the app/backend milestone.

Feedback inbox & publishing

Feedback submitted by beta testers is private by default. Only entries an admin explicitly approves should be published to the public WHO feedback page.

Preview workflow: submissions are received → reviewed → edited/redacted if necessary → approved or rejected → approved entries become public feedback cards.
