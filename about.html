About WHO

WHO is a communication product being built to make unknown calls easier to understand without turning your phone into a complicated dashboard.

Our direction

WHO combines a modern dialer experience with caller context, contact management, messaging and a future community identity layer.

Built in public

WHO is currently a beta product. The app, website and backend will evolve quickly as features are tested.

Created by Squashberry

The project is independently developed under the Squashberry brand.

Why “WHO”?

Because the core question is simple: who is trying to reach you?
