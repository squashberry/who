Scam Alerts

Clear warnings when a communication looks suspicious.

Signals, not certainty

Future WHO scam detection can combine user reports, reputation patterns and other permitted signals to warn about suspicious communication.

What WHO will not do

WHO will not guarantee that a caller or message is safe merely because no warning appears.

Protect your codes

Never share a password, OTP, banking PIN or recovery code with an unknown caller. WHO will never need your one-time authentication code through a random phone conversation.
