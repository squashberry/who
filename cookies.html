Cookie Policy

How the WHO website handles cookies and similar technologies.

Current website

The current WHO GitHub Pages website is intentionally lightweight. It does not currently require an advertising tracker or a user account just to browse public pages.

Essential technologies

Browsers may store ordinary technical state needed for normal web operation. If future features add login, preferences, analytics or embedded services that set cookies, this policy will be updated.

Third-party services

Links to app stores, Google, GitHub or other third-party services take you to systems with their own privacy and cookie practices. Review those providers' policies when you use their services.

Changes

WHO will not add non-essential tracking to this website without updating the relevant notice and documenting its purpose.
