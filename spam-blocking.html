Spam protection

Make unwanted calls easier to handle.

WHO gives the phone experience room for caller context, reporting and blocking without pretending that every unfamiliar call is spam.

Recognize

See the available context

WHO can surface caller signals when they exist, helping you distinguish a known contact from an unfamiliar or reported number.

Report

Send a useful signal

When a caller is unwanted or suspicious, use the in-app report flow. Community reporting can help improve future caller context.

Block

Take action on the phone

Use the Android call controls available to your device and WHO to ignore or block callers you do not want to hear from.

Stay careful

Do not trust a label blindly

A familiar-looking name can still be wrong or spoofed. Never share an OTP, PIN or banking information just because a caller appears familiar.
