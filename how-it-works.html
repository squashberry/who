How WHO works

Useful context before you act.

WHO combines phone features with caller intelligence and network tools. The app is designed to show context, let you verify what you can, and leave the final action to you.

Caller

1. A call arrives

WHO starts with the number, your local contacts and the caller signals available to the app.

Context

2. WHO compares signals

Known contact information can be combined with anonymized caller observations and other available reputation signals.

Explain

3. You see the result

WHO can surface a candidate name or reputation context with a confidence signal rather than pretending every result is certain.

Action

4. You decide

Answer, ignore, block or report. WHO is there to assist with context, not to make the decision for you.

Relay

5. Connections can be shared

WHO Relay pairs two WHO phones using a fresh session credential, allowing a receiving phone to use the host's available connection.

Learn Relay →

Network

6. Traffic can use WHO VPN

The native Android VPN/tun2socks path can route supported traffic through a selected WHO network route.
