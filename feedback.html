Beta 1.0

Every report helps us improve WHO.

We want practical feedback from real users. Tell us about the caller experience, contacts, SMS, permissions, Relay, performance, crashes, confusing screens, missing features or anything that would make WHO better.

Do not send passwords, one-time codes, banking PINs, private contact lists or other sensitive secrets through this form.

What happens after you submit?

  1. We receive your report or message.
  2. We review it for relevance, privacy and safety.
  3. Bug reports and complaints can be turned into internal fixes or support cases.
  4. Only feedback explicitly eligible for publication is considered for the public gallery.
  5. An admin must approve public feedback before it appears on this website.

Need private help?

For account, privacy, deletion or security issues, tell us in the form and we will treat it as a private support request rather than publishing it.
