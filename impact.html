Our mission

WHO Impact

The long-term goal is safer, clearer communication — especially when the number on your screen is unfamiliar.

Safer communication

WHO is intended to help people understand calls and messages before deciding what to do next.

Local relevance

The product is being developed with emerging-market communication realities in mind, including varied caller patterns, limited identity information and the need for low-friction mobile tools.

Trust matters

Good caller intelligence is not just a bigger database. It needs correction, transparency, privacy controls and visible uncertainty.

Beta impact metrics

Future public impact reporting may include aggregated measures such as spam reports processed, successful corrections, service reliability and active users. No private contact contents should be used in public metrics.
