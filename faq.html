Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about WHO, beta access and privacy.

Is WHO free?

WHO Beta 1.0 is being developed as a free-first product. Future paid or premium features, if introduced, will be clearly identified before purchase.

Does WHO upload my contacts?

The current product direction separates your private contact list from the future community identity system. If a future feature needs contact-derived observations, WHO will document what is collected, why it is needed and what controls you have before enabling it.

Can I back up my contacts?

Yes, this is planned. WHO will support user-authorized Google Drive backup and restore. The backup will be separate from the community caller-intelligence dataset.

Can I remove my information?

WHO is being built with correction, deletion and identity opt-out controls. See Your Data for the available beta controls and planned requests.

How do I contact WHO?

Email squashberrypro@gmail.com.
