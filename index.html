WHO Relay

Turn two WHO phones into a private little network.

WHO Relay lets one Android device share its internet connection with another WHO device through a local authenticated link rather than relying on ordinary phone tethering alone.

01Host or receiveShare mode and Receive mode are built into the Relay flow.
02Pair with a fresh codeEvery relay session gets a random pairing code.
03Route traffic through VPNThe receiving device can use WHO's native VPN/tun2socks path for the session.

Privacy

Control first. Intelligence second.

WHO's caller architecture is designed around consent, normalization, confidence and report/correction flows. The app can compare names from contacts with anonymized caller observations; raw phone numbers are not intended to be stored in the caller database.

ConsentExplicit contacts/call permissions.
MinimizationDo not turn raw personal data into analytics.
CorrectionCaller signals can be reported and challenged.
