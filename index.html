WHY WHO

Good phone software
knows when not to guess.

WHO is being built around a simple idea: useful information is better than false certainty.

01

Signals, not verdicts

Identity suggestions carry uncertainty. WHO should never turn a guess into a fact.

02

Private stays private

Your address book should not silently become a public directory just because you use the app.

03

You make the call

Answer, ignore, block or report. WHO gives you better context; the decision remains yours.

04

Separate by design

Backup, community intelligence and operational systems are treated as different jobs with different boundaries.

WHO RELAY
