FAQ

Questions people ask before using WHO.

Caller intelligence is useful only when people understand what it can and cannot tell them.

Does WHO know exactly who owns every number?

No. WHO can provide identity signals and community context, but results can be incomplete or incorrect. A match is not proof of legal identity.

Can I search a number from the website?

The public web lookup experience is being introduced alongside the WHO Android product. Live caller intelligence remains available inside the app.

Does WHO upload my private contacts?

A private address book should not silently become a public directory. Contact, backup and caller-intelligence systems are designed with separate boundaries.

Is WHO a VPN or anonymity service?

WHO includes supported network protection, but a VPN does not make a person anonymous or guarantee that every destination is safe.

Can I use WHO Relay between phones?

Yes. One WHO Android phone can act as Host and another as Receiver using an explicit Relay session and fresh pairing credential.

Where do I report a problem?

Use the public WHO Beta Feedback page. Never include OTPs, passwords or private credentials.
