Built for communication

One place for the things your phone should understand.

WHO is being designed as more than a dialer — it connects calling, contacts, SMS and caller intelligence into one consistent experience.

Smart Caller ID

Surface useful identity information when an unknown number reaches you, while keeping the phone UI fast and understandable.

Contact intelligence

Compare opted-in contact observations and build a confidence-based identity layer rather than treating one uploaded name as absolute truth.

Calls + SMS

Keep everyday calling, recent activity and messaging close together, with the same visual language across the app.

Safer communication

Leave room for spam intelligence, reports, blocking and later reputation signals without turning the interface into noise.

Restore without the panic

Back up contacts to your Google Drive and restore them when moving to another device, with user-controlled authorization.

Privacy by design

WHO will make consent, deletion, correction and data visibility first-class product decisions as the identity network grows.

The next intelligence layer

Names that get smarter with permission.

Future WHO builds can use an opt-in community identity system: a number may be observed under different contact names, and the service can compare those observations to produce a confidence-based result.

Multiple independent contact observations can raise confidence.
Conflicting names can be handled as competing candidates instead of silently overwriting one another.
Users can report incorrect identities and corrections can feed the moderation queue.
The goal is not to claim that every matched name is “the real name”. WHO should show why an identity is suggested and how confident the system is.
