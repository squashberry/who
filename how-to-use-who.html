How to use WHO

A practical guide to the WHO Beta 1.0 phone experience — from first launch and permissions to Caller ID, calls, SMS, Relay and VPN.

1 · First launch

Set up WHO as your phone app

Open WHO, review the permissions it requests and grant only the access needed for the features you want to use. On supported Android versions, choose WHO as your default phone/dialer app when you want WHO to handle calling.

2 · Caller ID

Check the caller before you answer

When an unfamiliar number calls, WHO can show available identity or reputation context. Treat a match as useful information, not automatic proof. You can answer, ignore, block or report from the call flow.

3 · Dialer & SMS

Call and message normally

Use Recents, Contacts, the keypad and call actions from WHO. SMS conversations stay close to the same phone experience, with spam and message intelligence expanding during the beta.

4 · Contacts

Keep your own contacts in control

Save numbers you recognize to your contacts. If a caller suggestion is wrong, use the available correction or report path rather than treating the suggestion as certain.

5 · Relay

Share a connection between WHO phones

Use WHO Relay when one Android device has internet access and another WHO device needs to use that connection. One device uses Share/Host mode and the other uses Receive mode.

Read the Relay guide →

6 · VPN

Protect supported traffic with WHO Network

Choose an available network endpoint, review its connection information, approve Android's VPN permission and connect. The app should show the protected/active state while the tunnel is running.

Read the VPN guide →

Relay note

Hotspot warning

If WHO tells you a normal Android hotspot must be turned off before starting Relay, follow the warning. WHO should not silently disable your hotspot; use the provided Android settings action and then return to WHO.

Updates

Keep the beta current

WHO may require an update for security or compatibility. When an update is mandatory, the app will guide you to the current release before continuing.

Quick safety tips

Tip 01

Never share an OTP, banking PIN or verification code with an unknown caller just because WHO displays a familiar-looking name.

Tip 02

Only pair Relay with devices you recognize and review the session state before routing traffic through it.

Tip 03

A VPN can protect traffic on the VPN path, but it does not make you anonymous or make every website trustworthy.
