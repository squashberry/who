WHO Support

Troubleshooting and account guidance for the WHO beta.

Before contacting support

  • Check that WHO is updated to the latest available version.
  • Restart the app after a major Android permission change.
  • Check that WHO has the permissions required for the feature you are using.
  • For caller ID issues, note the phone number format and device model, but do not send private contact lists.
