COMMON PROBLEMS

Start here before reinstalling.

Most beta issues are easier to diagnose from the exact state than from a generic “it doesn't work” report.

CALLER ID / CALLS01

WHO is not showing caller context

Check that the required phone/call permissions are granted, WHO is allowed to operate as the intended phone app where applicable, and that the number is actually available to the caller-intelligence service.

Do not assume that no match means a number is safe.

RELAY02

Receiver cannot see the host

Confirm both devices are using compatible WHO builds, the host is actually in Relay Host mode, the pairing session is fresh, and Android permissions required for nearby discovery have been granted.

If Android is presenting a hotspot UI, follow the WHO Relay warning rather than mixing the two connection modes.

NETWORK03

VPN connects but traffic does not work

Check the selected endpoint, protected status, network availability and Android VPN permission. Include the visible connection state and device Android version in a bug report.

Do not describe VPN problems as “internet is off” without the exact WHO state.

ACCOUNT04

Account says it already exists

Use the login/verification route rather than creating another account. The service should distinguish an existing account from a missing account instead of silently making a duplicate identity.

Never send a one-time code to support.

REPORTING A BUG

Give the team something we can reproduce.

Include these details

  • WHO version/build number.
  • Phone model and Android version.
  • The exact screen or feature.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened.
  • Steps that reproduce it.
  • Screenshot or video when useful.

Do not include these

  • Passwords or OTPs.
  • Account recovery secrets.
  • Banking PINs or payment credentials.
  • Private contact lists you do not need to share.
  • Desktop authorization credentials.
Support will never need your verification code to “fix” your account.
