Trust & safety

Community Guidelines

Rules for names, reports, profile information and other community contributions.

Be accurate

Do not deliberately submit false names, fake business identities, fabricated spam reports or misleading information.

Do not abuse identity data

Do not use WHO to stalk, intimidate, harass, dox or expose private information about another person.

No impersonation

Do not pretend to be a person, organization or authority that you are not.

Respect corrections

If a person disputes an identity label, WHO may pause, review or remove the disputed signal while the issue is evaluated.

Moderation

WHO may limit visibility, remove reports, restrict accounts or disable community features when needed to protect users and the service.

Appeals

Contact squashberrypro@gmail.com if you believe a moderation action is incorrect.
