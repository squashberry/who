Safety

Safety Center

Tools and guidance for safer calls, messages and identity decisions.

Spam and scam reports

Report numbers or messages that appear unwanted, deceptive or abusive. Reports can contribute to reputation systems and moderation, subject to the applicable privacy rules.

Don't trust the badge alone

Caller ID is contextual information, not proof that a caller is trustworthy. Never disclose passwords, one-time codes or financial credentials merely because WHO displays a name.

Corrections

If WHO shows an incorrect candidate name or classification, use the app's correction/report controls or contact support.

Security

WHO will use authentication, access controls, encrypted transport and least-privilege service credentials as the backend is introduced.

Vulnerabilities

Security researchers can report issues through the responsible disclosure page.
