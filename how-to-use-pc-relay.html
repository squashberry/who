Use WHO Relay with a Windows PC

WHO PC can receive internet through a WHO Relay phone. For older Windows Wi-Fi adapters, WHO Relay uses Ultra Relay compatibility mode.

1 · Host phone

Turn on Ultra Relay

On the Android phone that has working internet, open WHO → Relay and start Share / Host mode. Ultra Relay creates the compatibility hotspot used by older Windows PCs.

2 · Windows PC

Connect to the WHO hotspot

Open Windows Wi-Fi and connect the PC to the hotspot created by WHO Relay. Keep the phone close to the PC while the Relay session is active.

3 · WHO PC

Open Relay

Open WHO PC and go to Relay. WHO PC continuously searches the connected local network for the WHO Relay service. You should not need to guess a gateway address.

4 · Authenticate

Use the fresh Relay code

When WHO PC identifies the WHO Relay, enter the current pairing code shown by the host phone. Do not reuse an older Relay code.

5 · Connected

Use WHO Internet

After authentication, WHO PC starts its Relay tunnel and routes traffic through the WHO phone. Keep the host phone's Relay session active while the PC is using the connection.

6 · Stop safely

Disconnect when finished

Disconnect WHO Relay from WHO PC before turning off the host session. WHO should restore the PC's previous network path when the Relay tunnel stops.

Why Ultra Relay?

Compatibility mode for older PCs

Modern phones and laptops may support WPA3. Older Windows Wi-Fi adapters may not. Ultra Relay uses a WPA2-Personal compatibility hotspot so older WHO PC hardware can join the Relay network.

Ultra Relay does not replace WHO's Relay authentication. The Wi-Fi connection and WHO pairing session are separate layers.

Troubleshooting WHO PC Relay

PC cannot see the hotspot

Make sure Ultra Relay is running on the host phone, keep the phone nearby, and make sure Windows Wi-Fi is enabled.

Hotspot is visible but will not connect

Ultra Relay is designed for older Windows Wi-Fi adapters. Try disconnecting other Wi-Fi networks first and reconnecting to the WHO Relay hotspot.

WHO PC keeps searching

Confirm that Windows is actually connected to the WHO Relay hotspot. WHO PC cannot discover the phone's Relay service while Windows is disconnected from the Relay network.

Pairing fails

Start a fresh Ultra Relay session on the phone and use the newly generated pairing code.

No internet after pairing

Check that the host phone itself has internet and that the WHO Relay session still shows connected.

Still stuck

Send a report through the WHO feedback page with your WHO PC version, Windows version and the Relay screen status.
