Privacy Center

Your Data & Privacy Choices

Controls for correcting, deleting, limiting or opting out of WHO data use.

Request help with your information

WHO is being designed to support clear privacy requests. Until the automated Privacy Center is connected to the backend, email squashberrypro@gmail.com with the request type below.

Access

Request a description or copy of personal information associated with your WHO account, where applicable.

Correction

Tell us that a profile, caller name or other data is inaccurate.

Deletion

Request deletion of personal information where applicable and subject to legally required exceptions.

Unlist / opt out

When the community caller-intelligence directory is launched, WHO intends to provide a direct way to remove your number from relevant search or identity results.

Revoke permissions

You can revoke permissions in your device settings. Some features may stop working after permissions are removed.

Google Drive

You can revoke WHO's Google authorization from your Google Account. Removing authorization does not automatically delete an existing backup file; you may need to delete that backup separately from Drive.

Community identity

Future community matching will require documented controls for correction, dispute, and opt-out. Those controls will be published before the feature is generally available.

For security, never email WHO a password, verification code, recovery code or private encryption key.
