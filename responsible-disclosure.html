Responsible Disclosure

A clear route for reporting vulnerabilities in WHO.

Report privately

Email squashberrypro@gmail.com with a concise description of the issue.

Please include

  • What is affected.
  • Steps to reproduce.
  • Potential impact.
  • Any proof-of-concept needed to verify the issue.

Please avoid

  • Accessing or modifying another person's data.
  • Deleting production information.
  • Denial-of-service activity.
  • Public disclosure before we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate.

What happens next

We will review the report, validate the issue and coordinate a fix where appropriate. Response timing may vary during the beta.

Scope

Only test systems or accounts you own or are explicitly authorized to test.
